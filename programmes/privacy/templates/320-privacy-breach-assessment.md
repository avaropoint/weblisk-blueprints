## The test — real risk of significant harm

**Sensitivity of the information**

*How sensitive is it, in this context and in combination with anything else that
was exposed? A name alone is different from a name with a date of birth and a
SIN.*

**Probability of misuse**

*Who has it? Was it recovered? Was it targeted or accidental? Is there evidence
of access? How long was it exposed?*

> [!NOTE]
> Significant harm includes humiliation, damage to reputation or relationships,
> loss of employment or business opportunity, identity theft, and negative
> effects on a credit record — not only financial loss. Assessments that reach
> "no harm" almost always considered only the last one.

**Conclusion:** real risk of significant harm — yes / no / cannot be determined
(treated as yes)
