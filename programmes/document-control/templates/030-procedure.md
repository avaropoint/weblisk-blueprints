# Untitled Procedure

> [!NOTE]
> A procedure describes *how* to carry out a policy or standard. Keep steps
> concrete and verifiable.

## Prerequisites

- Access / permissions required
- Tools or systems involved

## Steps

1. First step — what to do and the expected result.
2. Second step — …
3. Third step — …

## Verification

Describe how to confirm the procedure completed successfully, and what record
that leaves.

## Rollback

Describe how to safely undo the change if something goes wrong.
