# Workplace Complaint Investigation Report

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This document will be read by somebody hostile to it.** A tribunal, a
> Ministry inspector or a court reads this report to decide whether the
> investigation was *appropriate in the circumstances*. Write it so that a
> stranger can follow what was alleged, what was done about it, and why the
> conclusion follows from the evidence.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Confidentiality is not secrecy.** Information gathered here is disclosed
> only as necessary to investigate, to take corrective action, or as the law
> requires. It is also not privileged simply because it is marked
> confidential — assume every line may be produced.

> [!NOTE]
> More than one may apply to the same facts. Harassment on a protected ground
> engages the Act and the Code at once; the investigation is the same, and what
> differs is the remedy and the liability.
