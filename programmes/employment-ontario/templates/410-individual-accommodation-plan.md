# Individual Accommodation Plan

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not record a diagnosis anywhere in this document.** What the employer is
> entitled to is the functional limitation, the restrictions and the prognosis.
> A diagnosis in a plan that HR, a manager and a payroll administrator can all
> read is a privacy breach that cannot be undone.
