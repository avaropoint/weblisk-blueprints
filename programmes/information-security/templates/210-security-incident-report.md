# Security Incident Report

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Report first, classify later.** If you are not sure whether this is an
> incident, it is — open the record and let somebody else decide. A record
> opened and closed as "no impact" costs ten minutes; a report delayed until
> somebody was sure costs the logs.

> [!NOTE]
> If **personal information** may have been involved, a second and separate
> clock has started. Say so in *Information affected* and tell the privacy
> officer today — the notification duty is assessed under the privacy breach
> procedure, not here, and it does not wait for this investigation to finish.
