# Worker Orientation Record

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Before the work starts, not at the end of the first week.** The period this
> record covers is the one where the worker knows least and is watched least,
> and it is where new workers are hurt. An orientation signed on Friday for a
> Monday start has recorded the wrong thing.

## What they were told

- [ ] What the organisation does here and the hazards that come with it
- [ ] Their right to know, to participate, and to refuse unsafe work — and how to exercise the third
- [ ] That reporting a hazard, an incident or a near miss attracts no reprisal
- [ ] Who their supervisor is and who to go to when the supervisor is the problem
- [ ] How to report an injury, an incident and a near miss, and how quickly
- [ ] Emergency procedures: alarms, exits, muster point, first aid, who is trained
- [ ] Personal protective equipment required, how it is fitted, and who issues it
- [ ] Hazardous materials they may encounter and where the safety data sheets are
- [ ] The tasks they may not do until specifically trained
- [ ] Health and safety representative or committee, and who they are

*Add what this workplace requires. Delete nothing without saying why.*
