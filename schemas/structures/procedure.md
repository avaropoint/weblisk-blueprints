# Procedure

How a piece of work is done, step by step, by somebody who was not in the room
when it was designed. A plan shares this shape
([why `structure` is not `kind`](../programme.md#structure-need-not-be-the-same-word-as-kind)).

The form is declared in [`../programme.md`](../programme.md#the-structures-a-document-is-checked-against).
