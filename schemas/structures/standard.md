# Standard

An organisation's own standard: the measurable requirements that carry out one
of its policies. Not an industry standard — those are declared by
[`../standard.md`](../standard.md) and adopted, never written in this shape.

The form is declared in [`../programme.md`](../programme.md#the-structures-a-document-is-checked-against).
