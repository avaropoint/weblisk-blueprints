# Standards

**Reserved. Nothing here yet.**

`standards/` is for **industry standards** — ISO 27001, ISO 45001, SOC 2, NIST
800-53, CSA B51, COR 2020 — the published criteria an organisation is measured
against.

## What used to be here

Project guidance for the Weblisk client framework: pages, components, islands,
theme. Those were never general standards — they are one framework's own
concepts, and they now live in
[`frameworks/weblisk/`](../frameworks/weblisk/README.md).

The word was spent on the wrong thing. `standards` means something specific to
the people this framework serves, and a compliance programme that cannot name an
industry standard cannot say what it conforms to.

## What belongs here

The **declaration** of a standard: its controls, their identifiers, what each
requires, and how conformance is judged. Not a tenant's adoption of one — which
standards an organisation is measured against, and how well it does, is theirs
and lives in their own repositories.

A programme in [`programmes/`](../CORPUS_SHAPE.md) cites the standards it
operationalises; a standard here does not know which programmes use it.

See [`../CORPUS_SHAPE.md`](../CORPUS_SHAPE.md) for the sequence.
