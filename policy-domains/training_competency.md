## What this covers

Whether the people doing the work can do it: what competence each role requires, how it is established, the record proving it, and when it stops being current.

## Why these requirements

A register, because the matrix of who-must-be-competent-at-what against who-is is the only artifact that can show a gap. Retention, because a competency record is evidence years after the person has left. A template, because attendance and assessment records are produced in the field. No policy: the requirement comes from the work, and a training policy tends to say training is important.

## How it goes wrong

Attendance is recorded as competence. Having sat through it and being able to do it are different claims, and only the first is easy to evidence.
