## What this covers

Continuing to operate without something: working out what the organisation cannot do without, for how long, and what it does instead. Recovery, resumption, and the exercises that establish whether any of it would actually work.

## Why these requirements

A template, which is unusual and deliberate: the deliverable of this domain is a test report, and an organisation that must invent the form each time will run the test and record nothing comparable. A register, because the dependency list is the plan — a continuity plan written without one is a narrative. A procedure per scenario, because the plan is used by people who did not write it, under pressure.

## How it goes wrong

The plan is never exercised, or is exercised as a discussion. An untested continuity plan and no continuity plan differ only in how confident the organisation is.
