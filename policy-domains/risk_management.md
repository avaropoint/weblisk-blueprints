## What this covers

Identifying what could go wrong, sizing it, deciding what to do about it, and watching whether that worked. The subject is the risk itself, wherever it arises — the same machinery serves a safety hazard, a supplier concentration and an unpatched server.

## Why these requirements

A register, because a risk that is not written in one place beside its peers cannot be ranked, and an unranked risk gets the attention its owner's seniority buys rather than the attention its size deserves. A procedure, because the scoring has to be repeatable or the ranking is noise. A policy, because appetite is a decision somebody has to have made.

## How it goes wrong

The register is written once, at the start, and never re-scored. A risk assessment with no review date is a photograph being used as a live feed.
