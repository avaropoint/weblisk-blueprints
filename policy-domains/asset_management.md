## What this covers

Knowing what the organisation has and who has it: acquisition, identification, assignment, movement, return and disposal. Hardware, media, licences and anything else with custody attached.

## Why these requirements

A register, because that is what this domain is. Triggered work, because the errors cluster at the two ends — something arrives and is never recorded, or somebody leaves and it is never returned — and both are events. No policy: an inventory is not a position the organisation takes, and requiring one produces a page saying assets should be recorded.

## How it goes wrong

The inventory is reconciled against the purchase ledger rather than against the floor. The items that matter most are the ones that were never purchased through it.
