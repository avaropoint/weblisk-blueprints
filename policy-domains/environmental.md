## What this covers

Harm to the receiving environment and to the public beyond the work: discharges, emissions, waste and excess material, spills and their reporting, and the conditions attached to any instrument permitting the activity.

## Why these requirements

Triggered work, because the obligations here are overwhelmingly event-driven — a spill, a load leaving site, a condition falling due — and a periodic sweep cannot express a reporting deadline measured in hours. Retention, because the disposal record is what establishes where material went, and that is asked long afterwards. A policy, because whether an activity is undertaken at all is a decision.

## How it goes wrong

The programme is written for the regime that mostly does not apply and silent on the duties that always do. Triggered filings are the visible part; the per-load record and the written procedure are the ones owed every time.
