    The record and its lifecycle. Checking that the organisation did what it said is compliance_audit's.

## What this covers

The life of a record after it is made: classification, where it lives, how long it is kept, on whose authority, when and how it is destroyed, and what suspends all of that when litigation is anticipated.

## Why these requirements

Retention, necessarily — this domain is the retention schedule, and a schedule without a stated authority per class is a set of habits with numbers next to them. A register, because the schedule is the register. A policy, because legal hold overrides disposition and that override has to be something an employee can be shown.

## How it goes wrong

Disposition is declared and never performed. A schedule that has never destroyed anything is not protecting the organisation, it is describing an intention while the actual retention period remains forever.
