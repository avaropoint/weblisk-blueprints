## What this covers

Third parties the organisation depends on: deciding to engage one, what the contract must require, assessing them before and during, and getting out. It covers subcontractors and onward flow-down as well as direct suppliers.

## Why these requirements

Triggered work, because reassessment runs off the supplier's own dates — contract renewal, certificate expiry — and a single annual sweep will always be either too early for most or too late for some. An approval, because engaging a supplier commits the organisation to somebody else's controls and that is a decision, not an administrative step.

## How it goes wrong

Assessment happens at onboarding and never again. The supplier that was assessed four years ago and has since been acquired is the one nobody is watching.
