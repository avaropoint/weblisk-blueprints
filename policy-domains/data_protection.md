## What this covers

Personal and otherwise sensitive information: what may be collected, for what purpose, on what basis, who it may be disclosed to, what rights attach to it, and what happens when any of that is breached.

## Why these requirements

Triggered work, because the defining obligations of this domain run on clocks started by an event — a request arrives, a breach is discovered — and a periodic sweep cannot express a statutory deadline. Retention, because holding personal information longer than the purpose justifies is itself the contravention, so a keeping period is not administration here, it is the control.

## How it goes wrong

The inventory of what is held is written from what the organisation intended to collect rather than from what it actually holds. The two diverge fastest in exports, backups and spreadsheets.
