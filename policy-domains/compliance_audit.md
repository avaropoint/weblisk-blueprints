## What this covers

Checking, independently of the people who did the work, that the organisation did what it said: internal audit, management review, regulator and certification-body engagement, and the findings and corrective actions all of that raises.

## Why these requirements

An approval, because a finding nobody accepted is an observation, and management review is the moment the organisation formally looks at its own programme. A register of findings, because the value is in closure rather than in discovery. Retention, because the audit trail is what a certifying body asks for across cycles. No policy: what to audit against belongs to the thing being audited.

## How it goes wrong

Audit finds only what the auditor already knew. A programme whose audits never produce a finding the programme owner did not already have on a list is auditing its own paperwork.
