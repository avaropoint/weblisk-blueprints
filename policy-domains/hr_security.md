## What this covers

The person, as a source of risk and as somebody owed duties: screening before, terms and awareness during, and the removal of access, property and authority at the end. It covers contractors on the same footing as employees where the exposure is the same.

## Why these requirements

Triggered work, because every meaningful obligation here is raised by a life event — joining, moving, leaving — and none of them is periodic. Retention, because employment records carry statutory keeping periods, and those are set by somebody else. A register, because the leaver checklist is only evidence if it was completed at the time.

## How it goes wrong

The leaving process is owned by whoever noticed. Termination is the highest-risk moment in this domain and the one most often handled by an email.
