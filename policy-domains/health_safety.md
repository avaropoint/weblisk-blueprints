## What this covers

Protecting people from harm arising out of the work: hazard identification and assessment, safe work practices, protective equipment, worker participation, supervision and competence, and the response when somebody is hurt or nearly is.

## Why these requirements

The longest list in this family, and every entry is load-bearing. A template, because the daily and pre-task records — the hazard assessment, the toolbox talk, the inspection — are filled in on site by people who will not be drafting a form. Triggered work, because incident, exposure and equipment obligations all run off dates on records rather than off a calendar. Retention, because exposure records carry keeping periods measured in decades and set by statute.

## How it goes wrong

The programme is documented and not operating. The documented-versus-operating gap is wider here than anywhere else, because the paperwork is producible from a desk and the work is not.
