  health_safety: >-

## What this covers

Places and the things in them: perimeter, entry, visitors, secure areas, media handling and disposal, and the environmental conditions the equipment depends on.

## Why these requirements

A register, because the visitor and key-holder records are the only evidence this domain produces that anybody outside it can check. A procedure, because reception is usually staffed by people who are not part of the programme and need to be told. A policy, because which areas are restricted is a decision.

## How it goes wrong

It is scoped to a head office the organisation no longer works from. Sites, vehicles, yards and home offices are where the assets actually are.
