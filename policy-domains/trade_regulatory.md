## What this covers

The permissions that make the work lawful: licences, registrations, certificates, permits, notices and filings held by the organisation, and the statutory tickets attaching to the people doing the work.

## Why these requirements

Triggered work, because every item here has an expiry date and the only useful obligation is one raised before it — a licence renewed the week after it lapsed is a contravention that was fully foreseeable. A register, because the expiry date has to be somewhere a clock can read it. Retention, because the lapsed certificate is what proves the work was lawful at the time.

## How it goes wrong

Renewals are tracked by whoever holds the certificate. A register with an expiry column and no obligation reading it is a list, and lists do not raise anything.
